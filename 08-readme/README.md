# Getting started with UProc

To use any of the above modules, you need to connect your UProc account to Make. If you do not have a UProc account yet, you can create one at [UProc](https://app.uproc.io/#/signup).

> **Find UProc on Make App Store:** https://www.make.com/apps/uproc

## Connecting the UProc service to Make

To connect your UProc account to Make, follow the general instructions [Connecting to services](https://support.make.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001990194.html#create-a-connection). When creating a scenario, you will be asked to provide an email and API key. To get your email and API key, log in to your UProc account (https://app.uproc.io/#/signup) and follow the instructions below.

Locate the left panel and click on **Settings**, and click on tab **Integrations**.

On the Integrations page, locate the **Api Credentials** section.

Copy your **Username** and **API Key (real)** and paste them into Make. Now you can continue and finish your scenario.
