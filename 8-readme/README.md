# Getting started with uProc

To use any of the above modules, you need to connect your uProc account to Integromat. If you do not have a uProc account yet, you can create one at [uProc.io](http://app.uproc.io/#/signup).

## Connecting the uProc service to Integromat

To connect your uProc account to Integromat, follow the general instructions [Connecting to services](https://support.integromat.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001990194.html#create-a-connection). When creating a scenario, you will be asked to provide an email and API key. To get your email and API key, login to your uProc account (http://app.uproc.io/#/signup) and follow the instructions below.

Locate left panel and click on **Settings**, and click on tab **Integrations**.

On Integrations page, locate **Api Credentials** section.

Copy your **Username** and **API Key (real)** and paste it into Integromat. Now you can continue and finish your scenario.
